Why Buffalos?
The name is not an accident. It is a standard.

The African Buffalo is one of the most feared and respected animals on the continent. Not because it is the biggest. Not because it is the fastest. But because of what it does when it is challenged — and what it stands for when it stands together.

🦬
Part of the Big 5
Not because it is the most glamorous — but because it is considered the most dangerous and unpredictable. Hunters feared it more than any other animal. It does not back down. It does not forget. It does not give up.
💪
Relentless by Nature
A buffalo covers ground every single day — regardless of the heat, the terrain, or the conditions. It does not wait for the perfect moment. It moves. Consistently. Purposefully. That is our standard in the field.
🛡️
The Herd Protects Its Own
When a buffalo is attacked, the herd does not scatter. They turn back. They have been known to chase lions away and rescue captured members. No one gets left behind. No advisor stands alone.
🦁
One Buffalo Can Hold Off a Lion
The competition is loud. They advertise. They have call centres, apps, and celebrity endorsements. We have something they will never have — a herd that shows up in person.
What this means for us
We Move Together
No advisor on this team is an island. When someone is struggling, the team shows up. When someone is winning, the team celebrates. The herd moves as one — in the field, in the office, and in the community we serve.
We Are Relentless
We show up every day — regardless of the weather, the rejections, the closed doors, or the difficult months. The buffalo does not wait for ideal conditions. Neither do we. Consistent daily activity is what builds a book. Not talent. Not luck. Relentlessness.
We Protect Our Clients
Every family we cover is part of our herd. When the worst day of their lives arrives, we have already prepared for it. That is what we do. That is who we are.
We Don't Run When Cornered
When the market is tough, when a client is difficult, when a month is not going to plan — we don't scatter. We turn around and face it. Buffalos don't retreat. They regroup and charge.
We Are Not the Loudest — We Are the Most Committed
Capitec spends millions on advertising. Discovery has the Vitality ecosystem. We are not trying to out-shout them. We are trying to out-serve them. Because deep, consistent, personal service is what builds lasting client relationships. And lasting relationships build lasting income.
"The buffalo does not ask permission to be powerful.
It simply shows up — every day — and does what needs to be done."
That is Team Buffalos.

Clawback timeline
Commission is recovered if the policy lapses. The longer the client stays, the more you keep.
Months 1–6
100% clawback — full recovery
Month 7

Month 24+
No further clawbacks on 1st year commission
2nd year commission: clawed back in full if the policy lapses in the first 12 months of the second year (months 13–24).
